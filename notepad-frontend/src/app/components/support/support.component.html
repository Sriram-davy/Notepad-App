Support & FAQ

How do I create a notepad?

Simply navigate to SlashPad and type any word or phrase after the slash (e.g., slashpad.com/my-secret-notes). If it doesn't exist, it will be instantly created for you.

How do I save my notes?

You don't need to! SlashPad automatically saves your content to the vault a few seconds after you stop typing. You will see a visual confirmation at the top of the editor when changes are saved.

What happens if I forget my notepad password?

Unfortunately, because of the way our authentication works, we cannot recover lost passwords. If you lose your password, that specific notepad is permanently locked. Please use a password hint when setting up a private notepad.
