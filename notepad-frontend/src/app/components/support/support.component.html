Support & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions

How do I create a notepad?

Navigate to the SlashPad home page and type any word or phrase (e.g., my-journal, sriramdavy). Press Enter or click "Open Notepad." If no notepad exists at that path, one is instantly created for you. The path you choose is the notepad's address — keep it private or make it memorable.

Does SlashPad auto-save?

Yes. SlashPad auto-saves your content to the vault approximately 2.5 seconds after you stop typing. You can also click the Save Note button in the toolbar to save immediately. The status indicator in the toolbar shows when a save is in progress and when changes have been committed.

How do I password-protect my notepad?

Click the OPTIONS button in the top toolbar, then select Password Protection. You will be taken to the security settings page where you can set a password and an optional hint. Once a password is set, your note is encrypted in your browser before it is saved — the server never receives the plaintext.

What if I forget my password?

Unfortunately, passwords cannot be recovered. Because of the zero-knowledge encryption model, we have no way to decrypt or restore access to a password-protected notepad. Always set a password hint, and keep a record of important passwords in a secure password manager.

How do I share my notepad?

Click OPTIONS → Share a Read-Only Link. A link is copied to your clipboard. If your notepad is unprotected, anyone with the link can view and edit. If your notepad is password-protected, a special encrypted share link is generated — recipients can read the content without knowing your password, but they cannot edit the note. The decryption key lives only in the link's URL fragment and is never sent to our server.

How do I download my notes?

Click OPTIONS → Download as Text File (or the EXPORT button in the toolbar). Your current note content will be downloaded as a .txt file named after your notepad path. This is your backup — use it regularly for important content.

Why was I logged out automatically?

SlashPad enforces a 5-minute idle timeout. If your session is inactive for 5 minutes, you are returned to the home page. For password-protected notes, you will need to re-enter your password on the next visit. This is intentional — it ensures private sessions do not remain open unattended.
