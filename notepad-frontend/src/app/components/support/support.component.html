Support & FAQ

Common questions, answered simply

How do I create a notepad?

Go to the SlashPad home page and type any word or phrase (e.g., my-journal, work-notes). Press Enter or click "Enter Sanctuary." A notepad at that path is instantly created. The path you choose is the notepad's address — keep it private or make it memorable.

Does SlashPad auto-save?

Yes. SlashPad saves your content automatically about 2.5 seconds after you stop typing. You can also click Save Note in the toolbar at any time. The status indicator shows when a save is in progress and when it's done.

How do I password-protect my notepad?

Click OPTIONS in the top toolbar, then Password Protection. You can set a password and an optional hint. Your note is encrypted in your browser before anything is sent to our servers — we never see your password or your unencrypted content.

What if I forget my password?

If you forget your password, your note cannot be recovered. We have no way to unlock it — your password never touches our servers. Use the password hint feature and keep your password somewhere safe.

Can I change the app theme?

Yes! Click the Theme button in the bottom-right corner of any page. You can pick from five themes — Obsidian Gold, Midnight Emerald, Crimson Noir, Arctic Slate, and Parchment Light. Your choice is saved in your browser and remembered next time you visit. Theme preference is stored locally on your device only — never on our servers.

How do I share my notepad?

Click OPTIONS → Share a Read-Only Link. Anyone with the link can view your note but cannot edit it. For password-protected notes, a secure link is created that includes the decryption key — anyone who opens it can read the note without typing the password.

How do I permanently delete my notepad?

Click OPTIONS → Permanently Delete Note and confirm. The notepad and all its content are immediately and irreversibly deleted from our servers. There is no undo.

How do I download my notes?

Click EXPORT in the toolbar or OPTIONS → Download as Text File. Your note is downloaded as a .txt file. Keep local backups of anything important — we are not responsible for data loss.

Why was I logged out automatically?

SlashPad has a 5-minute idle timeout. After 5 minutes of inactivity, you're returned to the home page. For password-protected notes you'll need to re-enter your password. This keeps your session from staying open when you walk away.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't collect personal information, don't use cookies, and don't run any analytics or tracking. Your note content is encrypted before leaving your browser. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
