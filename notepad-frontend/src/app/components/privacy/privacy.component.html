Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Security & Locking

When you protect a notepad with a password, your note is locked using strong, modern encryption directly in your web browser. This means your text is scrambled before it is saved on our servers, and it can only be unlocked when your password is typed in. We never store your password in a readable format, keeping your notepad private and secure.

2. What We Collect

We collect only the minimum info needed to keep the service running:

  • The URL name (like /my-notes) of your notepad.
  • The scrambled note text (if password-locked) or the plain text (if unprotected).
  • An optional password hint to help you remember your password.
  • Simple timestamps showing when the note was created or saved.

We do not collect details like your computer info, IP address, or tracking data.

3. Cookies & Sessions

SlashPad does not use cookies. Authentication tokens (JWTs) are held in your browser's memory only for the duration of your active session. They are never written to localStorage or cookies and are discarded the moment you close the tab or your session times out (5 minutes of inactivity).

4. Data Retention

Notepads automatically expire after a period of inactivity. Once expired, the data is permanently removed from our servers. You can also permanently delete a notepad's content by writing an empty note and saving.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We will never sell, rent, or share your notepad data with third parties. Your data remains strictly within the SlashPad infrastructure. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Sharing Links

If you create a read-only share link for a password-protected note, a special key is added to the link. This key allows the recipient's browser to open the note directly without requiring them to know or type your password. The key is kept private in the link itself and is not saved on our server.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please reach out via the Support page.
