Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

SlashPad collects only the text you enter into your notepads and the optional passwords you set. We do not track your IP address, browser information, or utilize tracking cookies.

2. How Data is Stored

The text content of your notepads is currently stored in plain text on our servers. Please avoid storing highly sensitive or personally identifiable information (PII). Passwords are cryptographically hashed using BCrypt.

3. Data Security

While we use HTTPS to secure data in transit, we do not employ end-to-end encryption. Private notepads rely on standard authentication to prevent unauthorized viewing. Do not use SlashPad for storing banking details or critical passwords.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We will never sell, rent, or share your notepad data with third parties. Your data remains strictly within the SlashPad infrastructure.
