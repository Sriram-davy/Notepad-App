Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We believe privacy should be the default, not an afterthought. Here's exactly what SlashPad does — and doesn't do — with your data.

1. Who We Are

SlashPad is an independently operated online notepad. "We", "us", and "our" refer to the operator of SlashPad. The service is not affiliated with any company or organisation.

2. What We Collect

We collect only the bare minimum to run the service:

  • Your notepad path — the name you pick for your URL (e.g. /my-notes). Used as a key to find your note.
  • Your note content — stored encrypted on our servers. We never receive or store your plaintext password or unencrypted content when password protection is on.
  • Your chosen theme — saved in your browser's local storage only. Never sent to our servers.

We do not collect your name, email, IP address, device info, browser fingerprint, location, or anything else. No cookies. No analytics. No tracking — ever.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for one thing only: showing you your note when you visit your URL. That's it. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose whatsoever.

4. Password Protection & Encryption

When you protect a note with a password, encryption happens entirely in your browser before anything is sent to us. This means:

  • We never see your password
  • We never see your unencrypted note
  • We cannot read or recover your protected notes — ever
  • Lost passwords cannot be reset — there is no recovery mechanism

5. Theme Preference

If you choose a theme (e.g. Midnight Emerald, Parchment Light), that preference is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

6. Data Security

We store your note content securely and take reasonable measures to protect it. However, no internet service can guarantee 100% security. Unprotected notes can be accessed by anyone who knows your URL path — choose a unique, hard-to-guess path and use password protection for anything sensitive.

7. No Third-Party Sharing

We will never sell, share, or give your data to any third party for any reason. We do not use analytics platforms, ad networks, or data brokers. Your notes stay on SlashPad's servers, period.

8. No Personal Accounts

SlashPad requires no sign-up, no email, and no account of any kind. Because we hold no identity information, we have no way to link note content to any real person.

9. Children

SlashPad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly store data from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the date at the top will change. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.

11. Questions

Have a privacy concern? Reach out via the Support page.
