Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

SlashPad is built on a zero-knowledge principle. When you protect a notepad with a password, your content is encrypted using AES-256-GCM in your browser before it is ever transmitted. The encryption key is derived from your password locally — we never see your password in plaintext, and we never see the decrypted content of a private notepad. Your secret is yours alone.

2. What We Collect

SlashPad collects only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • The URL path (e.g., /your-name) you use to identify your notepad.
  • The encrypted ciphertext of your content (for private notes) or the plain text (for public notes you have not password-protected).
  • An optional password hint you choose to set — this is stored as plain text so treat it as a non-secret reminder.
  • Timestamps for creation, last save, and expiry.

We do not collect your IP address for identification, browser fingerprints, device information, or usage analytics.

3. Cookies & Sessions

SlashPad does not use cookies. Authentication tokens (JWTs) are held in your browser's memory only for the duration of your active session. They are never written to localStorage or cookies and are discarded the moment you close the tab or your session times out (5 minutes of inactivity).

4. Data Retention

Notepads automatically expire after a period of inactivity. Once expired, the data is permanently removed from our servers. You can also permanently delete a notepad's content by writing an empty note and saving.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We will never sell, rent, or share your notepad data with third parties. Your data remains strictly within the SlashPad infrastructure. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Sharing Links

When you use the "Share a Read-Only Link" feature on a password-protected note, the decryption key is embedded only in the URL fragment (the #... portion after the URL). This fragment is never sent to our server by the browser. Only the person with the full share link can decrypt the content.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please reach out via the Support page.
